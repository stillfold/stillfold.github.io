# Privacy Policy

**Calendar Transfer**, by stillfold
Last updated: 12 September 2026

## The short version

Your calendar never leaves your phone. The app copies events from one calendar
on your device to another calendar on the same device. There is no server, no
account to create, and no analytics.

## What the app reads

With your permission, it reads the calendars and events already on your phone,
through Android's standard calendar provider. It reads event titles, times,
descriptions, locations, reminders, colours and recurrence rules, because those
are the things it has to copy.

## What the app writes

With your permission, it creates events in the destination calendar you choose,
and nowhere else. It does not modify or delete anything in the calendar you are
copying from.

If the destination is a synced account, that account's own software then uploads
those events, exactly as it would for an event you typed in by hand. That upload
is performed by Android and the account provider, not by this app.

## What is sent anywhere

Nothing about your calendar, automatically, ever.

The app requests internet access for one reason: Google Play Billing, which
handles the purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play, and this app never
sees your payment details.

There is no analytics library, no crash reporting library, no advertising
identifier and no tracking of any kind.

## Problem reports

If you choose to send a problem report, it contains a description of the
*structure* of your calendar and nothing of its content: counts of events,
timezone names, which recurrence fields are used, duration formats, colour
values, your phone model and Android version, and the types of the accounts
involved.

It contains no event titles, no descriptions, no locations, no dates and no
account names or addresses. The whole report is shown to you before you send it,
it is short enough to read in full, and you choose where it goes using your
phone's normal share menu. Nothing is sent unless you do this.

## What the app keeps

After each copy the app keeps a small record on the phone so the copy can be
taken back: the date, the names of the two calendars, how many events it
wrote, which rows in the destination they are, and a fingerprint of each row
as it was written, so that a row you have since changed is recognised and
left alone. The fingerprint is a hash: nothing about the event can be read
back out of it. The record holds no event titles, descriptions, locations or
times. It lives in the app's private storage, is never sent anywhere, and is
removed with the app.

## Permissions

- **Read calendar**: to read the events you are copying.
- **Write calendar**: to create them in the destination you choose.
- **Internet**: used solely by Google Play Billing.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Data retention and deletion

Beyond the record of copies described above, the app stores nothing, and
that record is deleted with the app. Events already copied belong to
your calendar account and are managed there, not here.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the previous
versions remain in the project's history.
