# Privacy Policy

**Pickaxe Planet: Idle Miner**, by stillfold
Last updated: 28 October 2026

## The short version

We do not run servers and we do not collect anything ourselves. Your progress
is saved on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in
your Play Games saved games so you can carry on on a new phone. Ads come from
Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

## Your progress

Your progress (depth, coins, upgrades, pickaxes, pets and settings) is saved on
your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless it is also in the cloud
(below). The save is signed so the game can tell if it has been edited; that
check happens on your phone.

## Google Play Games: cloud save and leaderboard

Signing in is optional. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a
copy of your progress in your Play Games saved games, so it can be restored on
a new phone, and posts your deepest dive to the game's leaderboard, where other
players see it with your Play Games name and avatar. This data is stored by
Google under your Google account; we cannot see it. You can manage or delete it
in your Play Games profile or your Google account, and you can play without
signing in. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one for
a reward. There are no forced ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent
fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and
the approximate location worked out from it, device and app information, and
how you interact with ads. In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first,
and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. Google's
policy on ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google
tells the game which items you own so they can be restored on a new phone. The
game checks Google's signature on each purchase on your phone; nothing is sent
to us.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example
"your pickaxe is forged" or "your bait box is full"). They are created and
delivered on the device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in
Settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
