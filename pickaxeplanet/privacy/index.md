# Privacy Policy

**Pickaxe Planet: Idle Miner**, by stillfold
Last updated: 16 November 2026

## The short version

We do not run servers and we do not collect anything ourselves. Your progress
is saved on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in
your Play Games saved games so you can carry on on a new phone. Ads come from
Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

## Your progress

Your progress (depth, coins, upgrades, pickaxes, pets and settings) is saved on
your device, with a short record of when you reached each milestone (the Pace
section of Stats shows it). Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless it is
also in the cloud (below). The save is signed so the game can tell if it has
been edited; that check happens on your phone.

## Google Play Games: cloud save and leaderboards

You can play without Play Games. If you have a Play Games profile, Google
signs you in when the game starts; if you do not, the game asks only when you
tap Cloud save in Settings. While signed in, the game keeps a copy of your
progress in your Play Games saved games, so it can be restored on a new phone,
and posts your records (your deepest dive, and the distance of each Mine Cart
ride) to the game's leaderboards, where other players see them with your Play
Games name and avatar. This data is stored by Google under your Google
account; we cannot see it. You can manage or delete it, and turn off signing
in to games automatically, in your Play Games profile or your Google account.
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one for
a reward. There are no forced ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent
fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and
the approximate location worked out from it, device and app information, and
how you interact with ads. In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first,
and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. Google's
policy on ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google
tells the game which items you own so they can be restored on a new phone. The
game checks Google's signature on each purchase on your phone; nothing is sent
to us.

## Ratings

The game may ask Google Play to show its rating card after a win. What you
enter there goes to Google Play, as a rating given in the store does. The game
is not told whether you rated or what you gave.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example
"your pickaxe is forged" or "your bait box is full"). They are created and
delivered on the device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in
Settings.

## If you write to us

Settings > Support copies a support code that you can paste into a message
to us. It holds a few facts about your save (the game's version, the time,
how long you have played, your record, and whether the save looked edited)
and nothing that identifies you. It is encrypted so that only we can read
it, and it leaves your phone only if you send it. If you email us we see
your email address and what you write, use them only to answer you, and do
not pass them on.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
